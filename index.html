Background & Rationale

Chemical engineering is a central research area in addressing global challenges related to climate change, energy security, resource scarcity, environmental sustainability, and the transition towards circular production systems.

The International Conference on Emerging Trends in Catalysis and Chemical Process Engineering (IC-ETCCPE 2026) aims to provide an interdisciplinary platform for researchers, academicians, and industry professionals to exchange ideas in the chemical engineering field that enable sustainable, scalable, and resource-efficient technologies.

The conference will bring together fundamental and applied research spanning molecular and process-level engineering through reactor design, separation, process intensification, energy systems, biotechnology, resource recovery, carbon management, and systems-level assessment.

Mission & Goals

Objectives of IC-ETCCPE 2026

01

To present the latest developments in emerging chemical engineering and process technologies.

02

To bring together researchers from chemical engineering, energy, materials science, AI/ML, and environmental engineering.

03

To connect research with practical applications, process development, scale-up, and industrial use.

04

To encourage collaborations between universities, research organizations, and industrial leaders.

05

To provide opportunities for students and early-career researchers to present their work and build professional networks.

🏛

Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur

IIT Jodhpur, established in 2008, is an Institute of National Importance dedicated to excellence in education, research, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration. Located in Rajasthan, a region characterized by arid and semi-arid climatic conditions, the institute actively engages in developing sustainable and technology-driven solutions to address regional and global challenges.

⚗

Department of Chemical Engineering

The Department of Chemical Engineering serves as the organizing department for IC-ETCCPE 2026. The Department is committed to developing a new generation of chemical engineering education and research that combines fundamentals with emerging technologies. The Department fosters active collaborations with DRDO, the Ministry of Jal Shakti, and the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA).

Core Scientific Focus

Themes of IC-ETCCPE 2026

🔬
01

Catalysis for Sustainable Processes

  • Nanomaterials, porous materials, and membranes
  • Catalysts and catalyst supports
  • Functional materials
  • Materials for energy and environmental applications
⚡
02

Green Energy and Hydrogen Technologies

  • Hydrogen production and storage, fuel cells
  • Electrochemical processes and renewable fuels
  • CO₂ capture, conversion, and utilization
  • Carbon mineralization and catalytic CO₂ valorization
♻
03

Waste-to-Wealth and Circular Economy

  • Waste valorization and biomass conversion
  • Plastic and polymer recycling
  • Industrial waste utilization and environmental remediation
  • Resource recovery and life cycle assessment (LCA)
🤖
04

AI/ML and Digitalization in Process Industries

  • Artificial intelligence, machine learning, and digital twins
  • Data-driven modelling and simulation
  • Process monitoring, optimization, and control
  • Predictive analytics and techno-economic analysis
